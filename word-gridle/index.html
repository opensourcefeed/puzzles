Word Gridle Daily: today's word grid puzzle

Today's grid hides six words of two to six letters, and everyone gets the same six. Fill every row within 20 guesses, using only the letters lit on the keyboard. The next grid starts at midnight in your local time. Not enough for one day? Play Word Gridle Unlimited.

How to play

  1. Type a word with the lit keys. The keyboard offers only the letters the grid still needs, as many times as it needs them.
  2. Submit it. A hidden word fills its row. A word that isn't in the dictionary is free: it doesn't use a guess.
  3. Any other real word is a bonus word. It uses a guess, and each of its letters that matches a hidden word's letter in the same position appears in the grid.
  4. Reveal every letter of a row and that word counts as found, even if you never typed it.
  5. Find all six words before your 20 guesses run out.

Stars, streaks and past grids

  • Stars: solve in 6 guesses or fewer for three stars, in 13 or fewer for two, and in any more for one.
  • Streak: counts the daily grids you win in a row. A lost grid sets it back to zero.
  • Share: post your result as a grid of green and black squares, without giving away the words.
  • Past grids: every grid since puzzle #1 on 27 June 2022 is in the Word Gridle archive.

Frequently asked questions

When does the next Word Gridle Daily start?

At midnight in your own time zone. Each grid has a number, counted from puzzle #1 on 27 June 2022, so you can compare results with friends.

Do wrong words use up my guesses?

Only real words do. A word that isn't in the dictionary is free, and so is a word you have already found. A real word that isn't hidden uses a guess but still reveals any letters it shares, in the same position, with the hidden words.

How do I get a hint?

After three guesses in a row that don't find a hidden word, a hint button appears at the top. Watch a short ad to see one of the words you still need, then tap it to enter it.

Can I play a grid I missed?

Yes. Open the Word Gridle archive and pick any day since 27 June 2022. Past grids are saved separately and don't change your stats or streak.
