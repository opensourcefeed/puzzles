How to play Word Tail

Word Tail is a free word chain game, also called the last letter game. You get a starting word, and every word you add must begin with the last letter of the word before it.

CAT → TAIL → LION → NEST

  1. Look at the last letter of the current word.
  2. Type an English word that starts with that letter and press Enter.
  3. Keep the chain going. Link 15 words to complete it.

Each word scores one point per letter, so longer words push your score up faster. Your best score is saved on your device.

There is a new daily puzzle every day, with the same starting word for everyone and a streak to keep. Want more? Word Tail Unlimited lets you play as many games as you like.

Tips for a higher score

  • Reach for long words: a 10-letter word is worth twice a 5-letter one.
  • Avoid ending on hard letters like X, Q or J unless you know a word that starts with them.
  • Words ending in common starting letters such as S, T or E keep your options open.

Frequently asked questions

What is Word Tail?

Word Tail is a free online word chain game. Each word you enter must start with the last letter of the previous word. Link 15 words to complete the chain.

When does the daily puzzle change?

Every day at midnight in your time zone. Everyone gets the same starting word that day, and you get one try. Word Tail Unlimited lets you play as many games as you like.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row on which you complete the daily chain. Giving up or missing a day resets it to zero.

How is the score calculated?

Every accepted word scores one point per letter, so longer words score more. Your best score is saved in your browser.

Which words are allowed?

Common English dictionary words. Names, places, brands and abbreviations are not accepted, and each word can be used only once per game.

Is Word Tail like Shiritori?

Yes. Shiritori is the classic Japanese word chain game, also known as the last letter game. Word Tail is an English version you play solo, with a score and a 15-word goal.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Word Tail is free and needs no sign-up. It works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.
