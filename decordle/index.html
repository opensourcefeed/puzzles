Decordle FAQ

Quick answers to the most common gameplay and sync questions.

What is Decordle?

Decordle is a daily word puzzle where you solve 10 hidden 5-letter words in 16 total guesses.

How does one guess affect all boards?

Every guess is checked against each unfinished word, so a single guess can reveal useful clues across multiple boards at once.

What do the tile colors mean?

Green means the letter is in the correct position, yellow means the letter is in the word but in a different position, and gray means the letter is not in that word.

Do I need to sign in to play?

No. You can play in local-only mode without signing in. Google sign-in is optional and is only needed if you want to sync progress, settings, stats, and streaks across devices.

Can I continue the same puzzle on another device?

Yes. If you sign in with Google, your current daily progress can be restored on another device after you confirm the newer cloud save.

When does the daily puzzle reset?

The game generates a new daily challenge each day. Once the next daily puzzle is available, today’s board is replaced by the new one.

What happens if I get stuck?

You can use the hint button to unlock help for the next unfinished word, and the Help section explains how to use color clues more effectively.
