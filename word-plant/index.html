Word Plant Daily: today's hangman word puzzle

Everyone gets the same hidden five-letter word each day. Guess it one letter at a time: every right letter grows your tree, and every wrong one costs a leaf. You have 8 leaves, and a new word starts at midnight in your local time. Want more games today? Play Word Plant Practice.

How to play

  1. Pick a letter on the keyboard, or type it.
  2. A right letter appears in every place it fits, and your plant grows.
  3. A wrong letter drops one of your 8 leaves (5 in hard mode).
  4. Find the whole word before your last leaf falls, and the plant blooms.

Every day's game

  • Streaks and statistics: a win-rate ring, a chart of your wrong guesses and a calendar of the days you played.
  • My Garden: every word you solve plants a tree you can come back to, with its meaning.
  • Past days: missed a day? Play any earlier word from the archive.
  • A hint and a reminder: trade a leaf for the word's meaning, or add a daily reminder to your calendar.

Frequently asked questions

When does the new Word Plant come out?

A new word starts every day at midnight in your local time zone. Everyone gets the same word on the same day.

How many wrong guesses do I get?

Eight. Right letters are free, and each wrong letter costs one leaf. In hard mode you get five, and no hint.

What does the hint do?

It shows the meanings of the word, with the word itself hidden. It costs one leaf, you can use it once a game, and it can't take your last leaf.

Can I play past days?

Yes. After today's game, choose Play past days to pick any earlier date. Past days grow a tree in your garden but don't change your streak.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row you solve the daily word. Missing a day or running out of leaves resets it.
