Dordle Unlimited: guess two words at once

Dordle Unlimited is a free word game with two hidden five-letter words. Every guess you type is played on both boards at the same time, and you have 8 guesses to find both words. There is no daily limit: start a new game whenever you like.

How to play

  1. Type a five-letter word and press Enter. It is played on both boards.
  2. Read the colours. Green means the letter is in the word and in the right place. Yellow means it is in the word but in a different place. Gray means it is not in the word.
  3. Use the split keyboard. The left half of each key shows the left board and the right half shows the right board.
  4. Find both words within 8 guesses. Once one board is solved, keep going on the other.

Frequently asked questions

What is Dordle?

Dordle is a word game where you guess two hidden five-letter words at the same time. Every guess is played on both boards, and you have 8 guesses to find both words.

How many games can I play?

As many as you like. Dordle Unlimited picks two new words for every game, so you never have to wait for tomorrow's puzzle.

What do the split colours on the keyboard mean?

The left half of a key shows how that letter scored on the left board, and the right half shows the right board.

Is Dordle Unlimited free?

Yes. It is free to play in any browser with no sign-up, and there is also an Android app.

Is there a colour-blind mode?

Yes. Turn on High Contrast Mode in Settings to use orange and blue instead of green and yellow.
