Lexicade Journey: brain training word levels

Climb 60 word cascade levels at your own pace. Each level has a goal and a move limit: reach a score, make a long word, hunt for a letter, find hinted words, or solve words from their clues. Later levels add frozen tiles, stones and chains. Boards grow from 5×5 to 8×8, new tiles drop in from the top, and you earn up to three stars on every level.

For one shared grid a day, play today's Lexicade. The full rules are on the how to play page.

How to play

  1. Draw a line through neighbouring letters: across, up, down or diagonally. A word needs at least three letters, and a line can't jump over an empty gap.
  2. Read it the way you draw it. A word reads from the first letter you touch to the last. Drawn the other way, the same tiles spell something else.
  3. Make a real word and its tiles clear. Prefer tapping? Tap the first letter, then the last.
  4. Watch the cascade. The tiles above the gap fall down and form new lines of letters.
  5. Learn as you go. Every word shows a short definition, and your Lexicon keeps every word you have found.
  6. Daily board: no new tiles fall in. When a column empties, the columns to its right slide left. If no common word is left, the letters shuffle, up to three times, before the game ends.

Journey levels

Each word is one move. Meet the goal before your moves run out. Then comes the Word Rush: the game blasts the best words on the board by itself, five at a time with a shuffle in between, up to three shuffles, and their points count toward your stars. Out of moves first? A Last chance round plays five words for you, and if they meet the goal, the level is cleared. Tap the board to skip to the end.

  • Score, long word and letter hunt: reach a score, make words of a given length, or clear a number of tiles of one letter.
  • Word hints: up to three words on the board are listed. Find the ones you need.
  • Clue hunt: you see a definition instead of the word. Easier clue levels also show the first letter and the length.
  • Special tiles: a 5-letter word leaves a Line tile that clears its row, a 6-letter word a Burst tile that clears the 3×3 around it, and 7 or more letters a Wild tile that stands for any letter.
  • Special combos: draw a word through two special tiles and they grow stronger. Two Lines clear a cross, a Line and a Burst clear three rows, two Bursts clear 5×5, a Wild with a Line or Burst clears every tile of the letter it stood for, and two Wilds clear the whole board.
  • Obstacles: iced tiles can't be used until a word clears a tile next to them; stones can only be broken by a Line or Burst; chained tiles stay put until you use them.
  • Boosters: Hint lights the first two letters of a word, Shuffle mixes the letters, and Burst clears any 3×3. Every three new stars earn another.

Why it trains your brain

Lexicade asks you to spot words in straight lines, think a move ahead about where the tiles will fall, and recall words you know but rarely use. Every word comes with a short meaning, so new vocabulary sticks, and the Lexicon lets you look back at every word you have found. Only common words count, so the game works for children and adults alike.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Journey level run out of words?

No. New tiles fall in from the top after every word, and if no word is left the letters shuffle themselves. A level ends only when you reach the goal or run out of moves.

What are clue levels?

Instead of a word you see its definition, and you find the matching word on the board. They are the best levels for building vocabulary.

How do I get more boosters?

Every three new stars earn a booster, in turn a Hint, a Shuffle and a Burst. Replaying a level only counts stars beyond your best on it.

What are badges?

Small goals, such as a 7-letter word, a chain of 4 or a week-long streak. A banner shows each badge as you earn it, and the Badges tab in your Lexicon lists them all with how to earn the rest.

Is Lexicade free?

Yes. Lexicade is free to play in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers, and it works offline once loaded.
