Word Gridle Unlimited: word grid levels with no daily limit

Climb a map of levels at your own pace. Every level hides a fresh set of random words, and clearing one unlocks the next. Grids grow as you climb, from two words of two letters at level 1 to ten words of up to ten letters. For one shared grid a day with streaks, play Word Gridle Daily.

How to play

  1. Pick a green level on the map. Cleared levels stay open for another go.
  2. Type a word with the lit keys. The keyboard offers only the letters the grid still needs, as many times as it needs them.
  3. Submit it. A hidden word fills its row. A word that isn't in the dictionary is free: it doesn't use a guess.
  4. Any other real word is a bonus word. It uses a guess, and each of its letters that matches a hidden word's letter in the same position appears in the grid.
  5. Reveal every letter of a row and that word counts as found, even if you never typed it.
  6. Find every word with four guesses for each hidden word to clear the level, or press Restart for new words.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Word Gridle Unlimited have?

There's no last level. From level 512 every grid has ten words of up to ten letters, and the levels keep going.

Does a level always have the same words?

No. The words are picked at random each time you open a level, so replaying it or pressing Restart gives a new grid of the same size.

Why are some keys greyed out?

The keyboard lights only the letters the remaining words use. As you find words, the letters they needed go dark, which narrows down what's left.

Does Unlimited change my daily streak?

No. Unlimited keeps its own progress, the levels you've cleared on this device, and leaves the daily stats and streak alone.
