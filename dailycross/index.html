DailyCross: a free, easy crossword every day

DailyCross is a mini crossword with clues written in plain, everyday language. A new puzzle unlocks every day at midnight, and each one takes a few minutes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and works on phones, tablets and computers, even offline once installed.

How to play

  1. Pick a clue. Tap a square or a clue. Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down.
  2. Type the answer. Letters fill in crossing words too. A word turns green when it is right and red when it is wrong.
  3. Use a hint if you are stuck. Check a word, reveal a letter or reveal the whole word with hint points.
  4. Keep your streak. Solve the daily puzzle on consecutive days to build a streak and earn bonus hint points.

Three ways to play

  • Daily: the same puzzle for everyone each day, with a shareable result.
  • Archive: catch up on any earlier daily puzzle. Browse past puzzles and answers.
  • Journey: 150 levels that grow from five-word grids to big, tricky ones.

Frequently asked questions

When does the new DailyCross puzzle come out?

A new crossword unlocks every day at midnight in your local time zone.

Is DailyCross free?

Yes. DailyCross is free to play in any browser, with no sign-up. You can also install it as an app and play offline.

Is DailyCross good for kids and beginners?

Yes. The clues are written in plain, everyday language, so the puzzles suit children, English learners and anyone new to crosswords.

Can I play past puzzles?

Yes. Open the archive from the date at the top of the game to play any earlier daily puzzle, or see past clues and answers on the archive page.

How do hints work?

Hints cost hint points: checking a word costs 1, revealing a letter costs 2 and revealing a whole word costs 10. You earn points by solving puzzles and keeping a daily streak.
